      <h1 class="field field--name-title field--type-string field--label-hidden">EU-UK Summit - 20 November 2026 - joint statement released</h1>

  

                                        <div class="node__meta">
    <div >
      <i class="e-wall-clock3" aria-hidden="true"></i> 20.11.2026
              <span class="meta-separator"></span> <i class="e-profile"></i><a href="/taxonomy/term/400376_en?s=3225" hreflang="en">Press and public diplomacy team</a>
          </div>
    
  </div>

            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-text-teaser field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><p>The President of the European Council, António Costa, President of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen and Prime Minister of United Kingdom, Andy Burnham, met in Brussels on 20 November 2026 for the second EU-UK summit.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
</div>
      
                <div class="copyright-container">
                  

            <div class="field field--name-field-hero-image field--type-entity-reference field--label-hidden field__item">  <img loading="lazy" src="/sites/default/files/styles/hero_inside_content2/public/2026/image_medium_resolution/EU%20UK%20Summit%20Room.jpg?itok=Z5I_kTxG" width="1089" height="726" alt="EU UK Summit " class="img-fluid image-style-hero-inside-content2" />

            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-text field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><p>During today's constructive summit, the three leaders agreed on a joint statement:</p>
<p><a href="https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16174-2026-INIT/en/pdf">Joint statement of the EU-United Kingdom summit 2026&nbsp;</a></p>
<article class="media media--type-image-low-resolution media--view-mode-full-content-width-embed">
<div class="field field--name-field-media-image-1 field--type-image field--label-visually_hidden">
<div class="field__label visually-hidden">Image</div>
<div class="field__item">  <img loading="lazy" src="/sites/default/files/styles/full_content_width/public/2026/image_low/Nov%2020%203%20leaders%202.jpg?itok=USjpNk7E" width="1117" height="745" alt="EU UK Summit " class="img-fluid image-style-full-content-width"></p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-media-copyright field--type-text field--label-visually_hidden">
<div class="field__label visually-hidden">Copyright</div>
<div class="field__item">
<p>European Union</p>
</div>
</div>
</article>
<p>The statement builds upon the outputs of the 2025 Summit:</p>
<ul>
<li>the <a href="https://www.consilium.europa.eu/media/1edla1hi/eu-uk_joint-statement.pdf">EU-United Kingdom Joint Statement 2025</a>;</li>
<li>the <a href="https://www.consilium.europa.eu/media/3w3hhlxz/eu-uk-summit_sdp.pdf">Security and Defence Partnership</a> between the EU and the United Kingdom; and</li>
<li>the <a href="https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/statement_25_1267">Common Understanding</a>, a political document agreed between the European Commission and the United Kingdom.</li>
</ul>
<p>In the <strong>Common Understanding</strong>, the European Commission and the United Kingdom committed to explore a number of areas where there was potential to strengthen cooperation between the EU and the United Kingdom.</p>
<p>Today's Summit Joint Statement welcomes the progress made in the delivery of the commitments from the Common Understanding and identifies new areas for cooperation.</p>

</blockquote>
